THE IDEA

About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking application designed for capturing ideas, reminders, and important thoughts before they disappear. It gives you a focused space to keep useful thoughts organized and easy to access.

GET STARTED

How to use QuickNotes

  1. 01 Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. 02 Type your note in the New note field.
  3. 03 Click the Add Note button.
  4. 04 Review your notes in the Your notes section.
